Design

Each floor turns one and a half degrees

The floor plates rotate as they rise, so no two floors look out at quite the same view. Balconies follow the turn, and every residence above floor 16 gets a piece of the water.

The glass runs floor to ceiling, held by slim bronze fins that shade the rooms from the low afternoon sun.
